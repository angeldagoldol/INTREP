## Philippine law these drafts assume

- **RA 7394** — Consumer Act of the Philippines. Covers defective goods,
  warranties and deceptive sales practices.
- **RA 11967** — Internet Transactions Act of 2023. The current e-commerce
  law: online merchants must be identifiable, disclose terms clearly, and
  the DTI can act against non-compliant sellers.
- **RA 8792** — E-Commerce Act, gives electronic contracts legal effect.
- **RA 10173** — Data Privacy Act of 2012, enforced by the **National Privacy
  Commission**. If you process personal data of a meaningful number of people
  you may need to register a Data Protection Officer with the NPC.
- **BIR rules** — you must issue an official receipt or sales invoice.

**A real difference from the EU/UK:** Philippine law gives no general
"change of mind" cancellation right for online purchases. The EU and UK grant
14 days. Since you ship worldwide, either offer one window to everyone (simple,
and a selling point) or state the EU/UK statutory right separately. These
drafts take the simpler route.
