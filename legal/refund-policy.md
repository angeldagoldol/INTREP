## Faulty, damaged or misdescribed items
Under the **Consumer Act of the Philippines (RA 7394)** you are entitled to a
repair, replacement or refund if an item is defective, not as described, or
unfit for its purpose.

Tell us within **7 days** of delivery for anything obviously wrong on arrival —
damage in transit, the wrong item, dead out of the box. For a fault that only
appears later, your rights under RA 7394 and the Civil Code still apply and we
will still help; the 7 days is how fast we ask you to flag the obvious, not a
deadline on your rights.

Either way **return shipping is on us**, and so is the replacement's shipping.

Keep the packaging and send photographs where you can — it speeds up the claim
with the manufacturer.

## Change of mind
Philippine law does not require a change-of-mind return for online purchases.
We offer one anyway, **{{RETURN_WINDOW}} days from delivery, to every customer
wherever you are**, provided the item is unused, complete and in its original
packaging.

For a change-of-mind return **you pay the return shipping**; we refund what
you paid for the item, including the original delivery charge. Use a tracked
service — until it reaches us it is still your parcel.

## Buyers in the EU and UK
If you are a consumer in the EU or UK you have a **statutory 14-day right** to
cancel from the day you receive the goods, without giving a reason. Our
{{RETURN_WINDOW}}-day window above is at least as long and applies to you the
same way, so you never need to argue about which one you get. We refund within
14 days of receiving the goods back, or of your proof that you sent them.

## Your refund
Refunds go to the original payment method within **7–14 banking days** of us
receiving the item. GCash and Maya refunds usually land sooner than card
refunds; card refunds can take a further few days to appear, depending on your
bank.

## Warranty
Products carry the manufacturer's warranty. We will help you claim under it.

For **parallel-imported** items the brand's Philippine distributor is not
obliged to service them, so we honour the equivalent warranty period
ourselves — bring the claim to us, not to the local service centre. Either way
this does not replace your rights under RA 7394.

## Exceptions
There is no change-of-mind return on:

- clothing that has been worn, washed or had its tags removed;
- items returned incomplete — missing cables, chargers, manuals or packaging;
- damage caused by misuse, unauthorised repair, or liquid;
- vehicles, which are not sold through this website at all (see the Terms).

None of these exceptions apply to a **faulty** item. Your RA 7394 rights stand
regardless of the condition of the packaging.
