## What we collect

**When you buy something**, through our payment processor:

- your name, email address and mobile number;
- your shipping and billing address;
- the items, quantities and amount paid, and which payment method you used.

**When you send a vehicle enquiry**, directly through the form:

- your name, email address and mobile number;
- the message you write and the vehicles you selected.

**We never see or store your card details.** Payment happens entirely on
{{PAYMENT_PROVIDERS_SHORT}}'s own pages. We receive a confirmation, the
amount, and for card payments the last four digits. For GCash and Maya we
receive only that the wallet paid.

We also note the IP address of enquiry submissions briefly, in memory only, to
stop the form being used for spam. It is not written to disk and not linked to
your order.

## Who we share it with

- {{PAYMENT_PROVIDERS}} — payment processing.
- **{{COURIERS}}** — delivery. They get your name, address and phone number,
  and nothing else.
- **{{EMAIL_PROVIDER}}** — delivering the order notification to our own inbox.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising.

## Transfers outside the Philippines
Our payment and email providers process data abroad. We use providers that
apply protections consistent with RA 10173. If you order from outside the
Philippines, your address is shared with an international courier in order to
deliver to you.

## How long we keep it
- Order and tax records: **10 years**, per BIR retention rules.
- Vehicle enquiries: **2 years**, then deleted.
- Support correspondence: **2 years**.

## Cookies and tracking
**We set no tracking cookies and run no analytics or advertising on this
site.** Your basket is kept in your own browser's local storage so it survives
a refresh; it never leaves your device except as the order you choose to place,
and you can clear it at any time through your browser.

Because nothing here is used for tracking, there is no consent banner. If we
ever add analytics or advertising we will add one that blocks them until you
agree.

## Your rights
Under RA 10173 you may be informed, object, access, correct, erase or block
your data, claim damages, and request data portability. Contact
{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and we will respond within 15 days.

Some data we cannot delete on request: BIR rules require us to keep the sales
record of a completed order for 10 years, so we will keep the invoice and
remove what is not part of it.

You may also complain to the **National Privacy Commission**
([privacy.gov.ph](https://privacy.gov.ph)), whether or not you have raised
it with us first.
