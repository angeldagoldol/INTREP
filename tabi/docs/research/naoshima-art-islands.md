# Research log — Naoshima & the Art Islands (`naoshima-art-islands`)

Written 17 October 2026 (`factsChecked: "October 2026"`). Existing brochure, rewritten from scratch,
then **live-verified on 17 October 2026** with web searches S11–S27 of a 35-search budget shared with the
Okinawa brochure (S1–S10 and S28–S35 are logged in `docs/research/okinawa-coral-islands.md`). WebFetch/curl
cannot reach outside sites, so evidence is the search results (titles, URLs and returned snippets), not
full pages read.

**Status legend:** **VERIFIED** = backed by at least one current source found this session (two or
more for anything surprising; official sources preferred) · **HEDGED** = wording softened or number
dropped because the sources were thin or conflicting · **REMOVED** = taken out of the copy ·
**UNSEARCHED** = descriptive, non-numeric copy (no price, hour, date or access detail) kept from the
editor's knowledge and the earlier independent review, not re-searched for lack of budget.

**`factsChecked` decision:** every price, hour, date and access detail now printed is VERIFIED (or
hedged to what a source supports), so `factsChecked: "October 2026"` and the tagline "Facts checked
October 2026 · Tabi Guides" stay. Main corrections: Chichu now charges **¥2,500 online on weekdays and
¥2,700 at weekends/holidays** (more on site), opens **10:00–17:00** (not 18:00 in summer) and **closes for
maintenance 12 Jan – 19 Mar 2027**; the Naoshima New Museum of Art (opened 31 May 2025) is ¥1,500 online.

## Nostalgic streets — 18 October 2026

The map page (`p8`, "Where it all is") was replaced in the same position by a photo gallery,
**Islands of old Setouchi**: real photographs of 懐かしい (natsukashii) places on the smaller islands
around Naoshima — a ruined Meiji–Taishō copper refinery, an island harbour and a tiny island village.
Each caption names the place and gives one short detail; nothing else is printed. The map's pins (Uno,
Miyanoura, Honmura, Takamatsu) and its ferry-time body text are gone with it; the place pages keep
their own `lat`/`lng`.
