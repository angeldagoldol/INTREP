# Research log — The Japan Alps (`japan-alps`)

Researched and written 17 October 2026 by Tabi Guides. Research was done through web search only:
outside sites could not be opened directly from the research sandbox (WebFetch/curl blocked), so
every value below comes from search-result extracts of the pages listed. This brochure shared one
web-search budget with another (`mount-fuji-five-lakes`): 38 searches in total for both.

`factsChecked: "October 2026"` is set. Every price, opening hour, date and access detail printed on the
pages is backed by at least one current source, and surprising values (Matsumoto Castle's fee rise)
by two or more. Values where sources disagree are printed as ranges or with "about" and are marked
HEDGED below.

**Removed from the old brochure:** the fictional publisher "Hida Trail Company", its invented
packages (¥142,000 / ¥228,000), the made-up testimonial ("Elise M."), the e-mail address, office and
hours, the `stats` page (Okuhotaka 3,190 m and Kamikochi ~1,500 m were not re-verified this pass) and
the Nakasendō (Magome–Tsumago) material, which is outside this brochure's five places.

Status key: **VERIFIED** = printed value matches the sources listed · **HEDGED** = sources disagree or
only one weak source, so the copy uses "about", a range or a looser wording · **REMOVED** = could not
be verified, not printed.
